Hardware setup
==============

The back bone of ORC-OS is a set up in the field that can record short videos
and that can process them. In this section we give an overview of hardware 
requirements, guidance how to assemble your own station and a full example 
of a station assembly including a parts list. The assembly of your own station
is not bound to a specific set of parts. We highly recommend getting your own 
parts in your own environment so that your setup is optimized for your needs
and availability of spare parts in your own environment.
